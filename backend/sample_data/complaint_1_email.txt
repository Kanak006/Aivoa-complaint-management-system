From: procurement@meridianhealth-hospitals.com

Hello Quality Team,

We are writing to report a serious issue with a recent shipment of your injectable product.

Our pharmacy staff at Meridian Health Hospital identified visible particulate matter (small
white flecks) floating in 4 out of 12 vials from Batch/Lot Number APX-2291-B while performing
a routine visual inspection before administration. The product is "Cefotaxime Sodium for
Injection, 1g/vial", manufactured with a manufacturing date of 2026-02-14 and expiry date of
2028-01-31.

This was discovered on 2026-07-20. The affected vials have been quarantined and have NOT been
administered to any patients. However, given this is a sterile injectable product, we are
treating this as a potential critical quality issue and request immediate investigation.
